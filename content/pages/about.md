Health Maxxing is a health magazine for people who would rather understand their body than be sold something for it.

We cover the whole of it: what to eat, how to train, how to sleep, how to look after your mind, and what is going on when a part of you stops cooperating. Every piece starts from the evidence and ends with something you can do.

## What you will find here

- **Articles** that answer one question properly, with the sources listed at the bottom.
- **Condition pages** that explain symptoms, causes, treatment and when to get help, without the panic.
- **Calculators** built on published formulas that say what their numbers can and cannot tell you.
- **An exercise library** and a **food database** for the practical bits.
- **Myth or fact**, where we check the things everyone repeats.
- **A community** for comparing notes, and a weekly newsletter.

## What we believe

Most of health is unglamorous. Eat mostly real food. Move often and lift something. Sleep enough. See people you like. Do not smoke, go easy on alcohol, and get the checks that catch problems early. None of that sells supplements, which is why you hear so little of it.

We also believe you can handle nuance. Where the evidence is strong, we say so. Where it is thin, mixed or still being worked out, we say that too.

## What we are not

We are not your doctor, and nothing here is personal medical advice. We cannot examine you, we do not know your history, and a web page should never be the reason you delay getting help. Please read the [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer).

We do not sell supplements, take payment for coverage or run sponsored articles dressed up as editorial.
