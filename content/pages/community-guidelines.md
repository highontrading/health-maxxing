---
title: "Community guidelines"
description: "The house rules for the Health Maxxing community: be kind, share experience rather than prescriptions, and bring sources."
---

The community exists so people can compare notes about looking after their health. These rules keep it useful and safe.

## Be kind

Disagree with ideas, not with people. No insults, harassment, hate speech or body shaming. Everyone here started somewhere.

## Share experience, not prescriptions

"This is what worked for me" helps people. Telling someone what they have, or to start, stop or change a medicine, does not. Nobody here can examine anyone else.

- Do not diagnose other members.
- Do not tell anyone to ignore or go against medical advice.
- Do not promote cures or treatments for serious conditions.

## Bring sources

If you state something as fact, link to where it comes from. Guidelines and published research carry more weight than screenshots, videos or "I heard that".

## No selling

No advertising, affiliate links, referral codes, supplement or coaching pitches, or fundraising. Accounts created to promote something are removed.

## Protect privacy

Do not post anything that identifies you or someone else: full names, addresses, phone numbers, medical records or photos of other people. What you post is public.

## Keep it safe

- No content that encourages self-harm, eating-disordered behavior, dangerous weight loss, or misuse of medicines or other substances.
- No instructions for obtaining prescription-only or illegal drugs.
- No sexual content, and nothing involving minors.

If a post worries you, use the report button. If someone appears to be in immediate danger, encourage them to contact their local emergency number.

## If you are in crisis

Please do not wait for a reply here. Contact your local emergency services or a crisis line. [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential services by country.

## Moderation

Moderators may edit, hide or remove posts, lock threads and suspend accounts that break these rules. We do not always explain individual decisions, but you can [contact us](/contact) if you think we got one wrong.

## One last thing

Posts here are members' own views. They are not checked for accuracy and are not medical advice. See the [medical disclaimer](/medical-disclaimer).
