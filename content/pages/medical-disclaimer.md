**The short version:** this site gives general information. It is not medical advice, it cannot diagnose you, and it does not replace a qualified health professional.

## Not medical advice

Everything on Health Maxxing, including articles, condition pages, calculators, the exercise library, the food database, newsletters and community posts, is provided for general information and education only.

It is not medical, nutritional, psychological or other professional advice. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a recommendation for you personally. We do not know your medical history, your medicines or your circumstances.

## Always talk to a professional

Speak to a doctor or another qualified health professional before you:

- start, stop or change any medicine or treatment;
- start a new diet, supplement or exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are taking medication;
- act on anything you have read here that affects a health decision.

Never ignore professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read on this site.

## Emergencies

If you think you may be having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not use this site to decide whether an emergency is real.

If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line now. [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential services by country.

## Calculators and tools

Our calculators give estimates based on published formulas for typical healthy adults. They can be wrong for you. They are not designed for children, for pregnancy (apart from the due date tool, which is itself an estimate) or for people with medical conditions. A result from a calculator is not a diagnosis.
