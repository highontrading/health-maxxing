We collect as little as we can. This page explains what that is.

## The short version

- You can read everything on the site without an account.
- We do not run advertising trackers or sell your data.
- Calculators run in your browser. What you type into them is never sent to us.
- If you subscribe or create an account, we store what you give us so those features work.

## What we collect and why

### If you just read

- **Page view counts.** We count how many times articles and condition pages are viewed so we know what is useful. The count is not tied to you.
- **Abuse protection.** To limit spam and automated abuse we derive a one-way, salted hash from your IP address and use it for short-lived rate limits. We do not store your raw IP address in our database. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

### On your device only

Some features use your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us:

- your saved reading list;
- your preferred units (metric or US) in the calculators.

Clearing your browser data removes it.

### If you subscribe to the newsletter

We store your email address, the date you subscribed and which page you subscribed from, so we can send you the newsletter. Every email includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

### If you create an account

We store your email address, display name, handle, profile sticker and a password. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes: we cannot see your password. A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is used only for sign-in and is not readable by scripts on the page.

### If you post in the community

Your posts, replies, likes and reports are stored, and posts and replies are public alongside your display name and handle. Please do not post information that identifies you or anyone else.

### If you contact us

We store your name, email address and message so we can reply.

## Cookies

We use one essential cookie, `hm_session`, and only if you sign in. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers that host the site and, if we use one, an email delivery service that sends the newsletter on our behalf, and only as needed to run those services. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Newsletter details: until you unsubscribe, after which we keep only a record that the address unsubscribed.
- Account details and posts: until you ask us to delete your account.
- Contact messages: for as long as needed to deal with your request.

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email. You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data by [contacting us](/contact). Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law.

## Children

The site is written for adults. Accounts are not intended for anyone under 16.

## Changes

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and its date.
