# Fact-check queue

Articles and conditions marked `factCheck: pending` were written from established knowledge
without a live source check (see CONTENT_GUIDE.md, section 2). This file lists the specific
claims their writers flagged as most worth verifying. When you verify a piece: confirm each figure
against the cited source, replace PubMed title-search links with the DOI or PMC link, set
`factCheck: verified`, and delete its entry here.

Pieces not listed here still need the standard check; they just had no special flags.
