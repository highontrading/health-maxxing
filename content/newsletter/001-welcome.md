Welcome to **The Weekly Dose**. Once a week we send you the most useful thing we have learned about the human body, and what to do with it. No detoxes, no miracle anything.

For this first edition, here is the whole game on one screen. Almost everything else is detail.

## 1. Move most days

The global target for adults is at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, plus something that challenges your muscles on two days. Any amount beats none, and the first few minutes are worth the most.

Start here: [How Much Exercise Do You Need?](/articles/how-much-exercise-do-you-need)

## 2. Lift something

Strength is not a hobby for gym people. It protects your bones, your blood sugar and your independence later in life, and two short sessions a week is enough to begin.

Start here: [Strength Training for Beginners](/articles/strength-training-for-beginners)

## 3. Eat mostly real food

Plenty of plants, enough protein, more fiber than you are probably getting. You do not need a named diet to do that.

Start here: [How Much Protein Do You Actually Need?](/articles/how-much-protein-do-you-need) and [Fiber: The Most Underrated Nutrient](/articles/fiber-the-most-underrated-nutrient)

## 4. Sleep like it matters

Most adults need at least seven hours. A consistent wake-up time does more for your sleep than any gadget.

Start here: [How Much Sleep Do You Need?](/articles/how-much-sleep-do-you-need)

## 5. Know your numbers

High blood pressure rarely causes symptoms, which is exactly why you should check it. The same goes for a few other numbers that are cheap to measure and easy to act on.

Start here: [Blood Pressure Numbers, Explained](/articles/blood-pressure-numbers-explained)

> [!TIP] This week's two-minute job
> Measure your waist and divide it by your height. Under 0.5 is the goal. The [waist-to-height calculator](/tools/waist-to-height) will do the sum for you.
