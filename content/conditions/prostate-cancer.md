## What it is

Prostate cancer develops in the prostate, a small gland below the bladder that makes part of the fluid in semen. It is the second most commonly diagnosed cancer in men worldwide, with about 1.5 million new cases in 2022.[^6] In the United States, about 1 in 8 men are diagnosed in their lifetime.[^3]

Many prostate cancers grow so slowly that they never cause harm, while others are aggressive. Modern care focuses on telling the two apart.[^1]

## Symptoms

Early prostate cancer usually causes no symptoms.[^4] A weak stream, frequent urination and waking at night to go are far more often caused by an [enlarged prostate](/conditions/benign-prostatic-hyperplasia), but they should be checked.[^4] Blood in the urine or semen, bone pain in the back, hips or pelvis, and unexplained weight loss can signal more advanced disease.[^1][^3]

## Causes and risk factors

- **Age.** It is rare before 40, and about 6 in 10 cases are diagnosed at 65 or older.[^3]
- **Family history.** A father or brother with prostate cancer more than doubles the risk.[^3]
- **Ancestry.** Black men are more likely to develop prostate cancer and tend to develop it younger.[^3]
- **Inherited gene changes**, such as BRCA1 and BRCA2.[^1][^3]

## Diagnosis

Testing usually starts with a PSA blood test. PSA (prostate-specific antigen) rises with cancer, but also with an enlarged prostate and infection, so a raised level is not a diagnosis.[^7] An MRI scan often comes next, then a biopsy, which confirms cancer and grades how aggressive it looks.[^4]

**Screening is a personal decision.** The US Preventive Services Task Force (2018) says men aged 55 to 69 should decide individually after discussing benefits and harms with a clinician, and it recommends against PSA screening from age 70.[^2] By its estimate, screening 1,000 men aged 55 to 69 for about 13 years prevents roughly 1 death from prostate cancer and 3 cases of cancer that spreads. The cost is false alarms, biopsies and treatment of cancers that would never have caused harm.[^2]

The American Cancer Society suggests starting that conversation at 50, at 45 for Black men and men with a father or brother diagnosed before 65, and at 40 for men with several close relatives affected young.[^3] The UK has no national screening program, but men over 50 can request a PSA test.[^4] [Cancer Screening, Explained](/articles/cancer-screening-explained) covers the trade-offs.

## Treatment

Options depend on the cancer's grade and stage, your age and health, and your preferences:[^1][^4]

- **Active surveillance** for low-risk cancer: regular PSA tests, scans and biopsies, with treatment only if the cancer shows signs of growing. In a UK trial of men with localized cancer found by PSA testing, about 3% had died of prostate cancer after 15 years, whether assigned to monitoring, surgery or radiation therapy, although cancer spread more often with monitoring.[^5]
- **Surgery** to remove the prostate.
- **Radiation therapy**, from outside the body or from radioactive seeds placed in the prostate.
- **Hormone therapy** to block testosterone, which fuels the cancer.
- **Chemotherapy and newer targeted medicines** for cancer that has spread.

Surgery and radiation therapy can cause urine leaks, erection problems and bowel symptoms, so side effects are a central part of the decision.[^1][^4]

## Prevention and self-care

There is no proven way to prevent prostate cancer. Supplements have not helped: in a large trial, vitamin E taken alone increased the risk.[^8] Staying active and not smoking still benefit your overall health.

## When to see a doctor

See a doctor if you have trouble urinating, blood in your urine or semen, or new, lasting pain in your back, hips or pelvis.[^4] [Prostate Health: BPH, PSA Tests and What to Watch For](/articles/prostate-health-guide) can help you prepare for a conversation about PSA testing.

**Seek emergency care** for new back pain with leg weakness, numbness or trouble walking, or sudden loss of bladder or bowel control, which can mean pressure on the spinal cord. Being unable to pass urine also needs urgent treatment.
